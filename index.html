    Sheet data
    iPhone Shortcuts: clock in and out from the lock screen

    Make two Shortcuts (Shortcuts app → +): Clock In and Clock Out. Each has one action, Get Contents of URL:

    1. URL: your web app URL from above
    2. Method: POST
    3. Request Body: JSON, with three Text fields:
      action = punch
      kind = in (or out)
      secret = your secret

    Then in Shortcuts → Automation, add Arrive at work → run Clock In (and Leave → Clock Out), or put them on the Action Button or a Lock Screen widget. Open Work Clock afterwards and the shift appears with the exact time you tapped. The app has to be open for sync to pull it in, and Auto-sync must be on.
